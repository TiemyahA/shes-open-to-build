# Build Your Ring

Build a single-page web app called "#OpenToBuild". Purpose: lets a woman upload her profile photo and download it with a hot-pink ring and text along the bottom arc, in the exact style of LinkedIn's green #OpenToWork frame.

Layout (mobile-first, one column on phones, two columns on desktop): - Headline: "Change your ring colour." with "#OpenToBuild" underneath in pink.
Subheader: "Green means you're looking for a job. Pink means you're (that girl) building one. Drop in your photo, download, make it your profile picture."

Check out https://shebuilds.lovable.app/ and use some branding from there. Make it: Step 1: Apply to Shebuilds. You don't need to know how to code. Let's just build, girlies! ✨ 🔨 ✨ link: 
Step 2: Generate & update your @connector:linkedin:"LinkedIn" profile picture to #OpenToBuild here!
Step 3: Call on your tribe, & let's build!!

Make a large circular canvas preview (displayed responsive). Create a controls card with (1) photo upload via click or drag-and-drop, (2) ring text chips: #OPENTOBUILD (default), #BUILDING, #IMADETHAT, #NOCODENOPROBLEM, #WHUT, (3) ring colour swatches: #FF2D78, #FF5FA0, #B6178F, #111111, (4) a big pink "Download profile picture" button.
